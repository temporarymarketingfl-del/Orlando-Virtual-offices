# Virtual Office Aggregator Design Guidelines

## Design Approach
**Reference-Based Approach**: Drawing inspiration from Airbnb's location-based search interface and TripAdvisor's comparison layouts, optimized for affiliate conversions and SEO performance.

## Core Design Elements

### A. Color Palette
- **Primary**: #548ea1 (Professional blue - 198 35% 47%)
- **Secondary**: #c4c4c4 (Neutral grey - 0 0% 77%)
- **Background**: #f0f0f0 (Light grey - 0 0% 94%)
- **Text**: #1a1a1a (Dark - 0 0% 10%)
- **Accent**: #548ea1 (Matching primary)

### B. Typography
- **Font Family**: Poppins for all text elements
- **Hierarchy**: Clear H1/H2 structure for SEO optimization
- **Weights**: Regular (400) for body text, Medium (500) for subheadings, SemiBold (600) for headings

### C. Layout System
- **Spacing**: Tailwind units of 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 (0.25rem increments as specified)
- **Border Radius**: 0.8rem consistently across all cards and components
- **Grid**: Responsive grid system for provider/location listings

### D. Component Library

**Homepage Components:**
- Hero section with location-based search bar prominently displayed
- Popular providers grid with card-based layout
- Newsletter signup with lead capture form
- Trust indicators and testimonial sections

**Location Pages:**
- Comprehensive provider comparison tables
- Filter and sorting functionality
- SEO-optimized content blocks (1200+ words)
- Strategic affiliate CTA placement

**Provider Pages:**
- Detailed overview cards
- Pros/cons comparison layout
- Conversion-focused affiliate buttons with clear hierarchy
- Feature comparison grids

**Blog/Resource Center:**
- Article card layouts for educational content
- Category filtering system
- Related content recommendations

### E. Navigation & User Experience
- Clean, professional navigation with location-based breadcrumbs
- Search functionality prominently featured
- Mobile-responsive design with touch-friendly interactions
- Clear affiliate disclosure and transparency elements

### F. Conversion Optimization
- Strategic placement of affiliate CTAs without overwhelming content
- Professional trust signals and provider badges
- Clear value propositions and comparison tables
- Newsletter signup incentives and lead magnets

## Images
- **Hero Image**: Large, professional image of modern office spaces or business professionals
- **Provider Logos**: High-quality provider logos in consistent sizing
- **Location Images**: Representative images of business districts/cities for location pages
- **Blog Images**: Professional stock photos related to business, entrepreneurship, and office spaces
- **Trust Badges**: Provider certifications and platform trust indicators

The design emphasizes professionalism and trust while maintaining the conversion-focused approach inspired by successful aggregator platforms.
